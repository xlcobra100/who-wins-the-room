By combining immediate reservoir lowering with accelerated temporary relocation, we cut risk by eighty percent today without locking people out forever. This is not ideology; it is a viable path forward that protects our budget, our neighbors, and our future before the snow melts.

**Closing statements: Reese Calder (B, TEC-PRA)**, 132 words

Jamie proposes a hybrid solution that fails on both feasibility and safety. My plan isolates variables: we commit fully to the €25 million spillway and permanent lowering to thirty percent immediately. This single, testable step delivers eighty percent risk reduction within one year without opening new lawsuits or freezing property markets. It accepts a known cost of reduced power revenue to eliminate an unknown catastrophe that could wipe out our entire budget. We do not gamble on failed protests or temporary fixes; we execute the only engineering solution that works today.
