My plan accepts difficult, manageable sacrifices to secure our safety and moral standing without freezing property markets or provoking lawsuits. We must lead with courage, proving that justice for Riverside and future generations outweighs short-term profit. Let us not choose convenience over the duty to protect our children from a disaster we can prevent today.

**Closing statements: Jamie Okafor (B, TEC-REA)**, 111 words

Morgan appeals to emotion, but our budget speaks numbers. Her plan accepts eighty percent less revenue immediately, forcing tax hikes on essential services before we even stabilize the utility. That is not courage; it is financial suicide. By lowering the reservoir now for twenty-five million euros, we stop the seepage without freezing property values or provoking a lawsuit from Ostbrück that would paralyze our economy. We avoid the four-year rebuild that leaves us bankrupt and powerless during the critical melt season. Do not gamble our solvency on a moral high ground that ignores the hard truth: we must secure the grid and the town's finances first, then address other concerns later.
